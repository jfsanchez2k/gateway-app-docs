This section allows you to manage settings for general aspects of trading.

Business

This part of the form configures the business information:

Name: Name of the business.

Address 1: Business address.

Address 2: Business address.

Status: State of trade.

City: City of Commerce.

Postal code:  Commercial postal code .

Telephone: Business telephone number.

Email Email of the sender of email notifications.

Invoice number validation (RegEx): Characters allowed in the invoice number field.

Email Copies To: Allows you to insert emails to send Email Copies.

Email Header: Email Header  from the merchant.

Email Footer:  Merchant email footer .

Email Language(EN ES):  Merchant email language dropdown .

SMS Template:  Merchant SMS Template .

Agreement: General  trade information.

Logo: Allows you to insert  a logo for your business.

Customer

This part of the form configures the customer information:

Require Account Number: Allows you to require an Account Number.

Require Phone Number: Allows you to require a phone number.

Require Email Client: Allows you to Require Email Client.

Require Customer Address: Allows you to require customer address.

Invoice

This part of the form configures the invoice information:

Currency(RD US): Allows you to select the type of currency on the invoice.

Maximum Payment Amount: Allows you to set the maximum invoice amount.

Require Existing Customer: Allows you to require an existing customer to invoice.

Allow Partial Payments: Allows you to enable partial payments for the invoice.

Allow Open Payment: Allows open payments to be made to the invoice.

Allow Due Date: Allows you to insert a due date on the invoice.

Automatic Invoice Numbering: Allows you to generate invoice numbers automatically.

Payment

This part of the form configures the payment information:

Payment by Credit Card : Allows you to enable payments via credit card.

ACH Payment: Allows you to enable payments via ACH.

Enable Wallet: Allows you to enable the wallet on the invoice.

Layout

This part of the form configures the theme by displaying a panel of colors to be selected on each line, such as:

Text Color
