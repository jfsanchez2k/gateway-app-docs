## Password Protection and User Responsibility

For the security of your account, it is essential to protect your password. Your password serves as the key to your personal information, and safeguarding it helps prevent unauthorized access to your account. To ensure a strong level of security, you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password and should take appropriate measures to protect it.

### Password Policy

To help secure your account, your password must meet the following requirements:

- It must include at least one lowercase letter (a-z).
- It must include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z).
- It must contain at least one number (0-9).
- Your password must be between 8 and 12 characters in length.

Please note that passwords that do not meet these criteria will not be accepted, as they may not provide adequate protection.

### Password Expiry

For enhanced security, your password will expire every 90 days. You will be prompted to update your password before the expiration date to ensure continuous access to your account.

### Your Duty to Protect Your Password

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential. Avoid sharing it with others or storing it in insecure locations. If you suspect that your password has been compromised, you should change it immediately. It is also recommended to use unique passwords for different services to further safeguard your account.

By following these guidelines, you help maintain the integrity and security of your account, ensuring a safe and secure experience.
