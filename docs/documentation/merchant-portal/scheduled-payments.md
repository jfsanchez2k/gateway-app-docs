In this option we can see the results of the execution of recurring payments We can filter by the different search fields:

![](https://files.readme.io/b8a3554f7e3d38c02d847f79126f126b9567ee10693df60a4a163512e8308303-1a7a261a28f1d7d650b9fe41db3d5e94fcdc6c10e651768d73a482ef1eb6711e-image.png)

<br />

Channel: Channel used in the transaction.

Product Type: product type used in the transaction (ACH or Credit card) in the recurring payment.

Product Number: product number used in the transaction (Account number or credit card number - the last four digits)

Customer Number: Customer identification number

Status: Recurring payment status, there’s four options: Not executed, successfully, completed and rejected.

Response: Response issued by the financial processor of the payment method.

Recurrence Id: Recurring payment identification number.

Period: Recurring payment period, there is five options: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual.

Amount From: start of amount range.

Amount To: end of amount range.

Date from: start of date range.

Date To: end of date range.

To view the detail of the execution of the recurring payment, press the ‘detail’ button placed in the options column.

![](https://files.readme.io/fb695283067a38f984052d2d1cf7edba6229aac470db33d08de240378cae987b-c5d76e42b44418e88a028ba74329a925d336ce28f05c82bdfba9523e8199fcf8-image.png)

New Management: this option allows to save a management performed on the recurring payment and a comment.
