In this option we can view and print recurring reports.

We can filter by the different search fields:

Date Type: Transaction date type, there two options: Active range, execution range.

Date From: start of date range.

Date to: end of date range.

Payment Type: product type used in the recurring payment (ACH or Credit card).

Status: Recurring payment status, there’s three options: Active, canceled and completed

Period: Recurring payment period, there’s five options: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and

annual.

Product Number: product number used in the transaction (Account number or credit card number).

Contract Number: Customer's contract number.

Recurrence Id: Recurring payment identification number.

Amount From: start of amount range.

Amount To: end of amount range.

Last Response: Transaction response message.

Card Expiration Date: Card expiration date.

Channel: Channel used in the transaction.

Once we have selected the filters we want to search for, we select the report we want to print and press the button 'View Report’ to generate the report.

The recurring information reports are the following:

Recurring Plan Schedule

This report shows a detailed list of active recurring payment.

![](https://files.readme.io/7979441e8823e2b5561b1e6179c67867520a8751438efe1753c44c16944c033d-0db0394a1e5c5848bf4d886f1e579b6e67345de080a85fb13d24035fa5194e6b-image.png)

<br />

Recurrence Id: Recurring payment identification number.

Customer Account: Customer identification number.

Customer Name: Customer full name.

CC/Bank #: product number used in the transaction (Account number or credit card number).

Period: Recurring payment period, there’s five options: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and

annual.

Status: Recurring payment status, there’s three options: Active, canceled and completed

Start: Recurring payment start date.

To: Recurring payment end date.

Total Amount:Recurring payment amount.

Quantity: number of executions.

Last Processed: date of the last execution of the recurring payment.

Status: Recurring payment status.

Recurring Transaction

This report shows the processed transactions of the recurring payments.

![](https://files.readme.io/cbcda28b06191cf541b0429ef5166a89052e667e25cf3e31dffaecef0ec58e6b-3b99d494ce53676bc0d6990f94b5df1abcd083603e2cfc523fcc52440bad3fc3-image.png)

<br />

ID transaction: Transaction Identification number.

Customer Code: Customer identification number.

Customer Name: Customer full name.

CC/Bank #: product number used in the transaction (Account number or credit card number).

Date Created: Recurring payment create date.

Last Processed: date of the last execution of the recurring payment.

Attempts: Recurring payment charge attempts.

Amount:         Recurring payment amount.

Channel: Channel used in the transaction.

Service: Recurring payment service that was paid.

Response Code: Transaction response code.

Authorization Number: Transaction authorization number.

Plan Credit Card Exp

This report that shows the information of customers who have recurring payments with credit card payment type that are about to expire.

![](https://files.readme.io/d5752d4953a783fee9ef521cc3b0606fd0eda58639a8561c5be9cfe52785a579-e287327bcd78500e5c0032d1996e6cb63630a08d890a566c7075badcd1e3020c-image.png)

<br />

Customer Account: Customer identification number.

Customer Name: Customer full name.

Credit Card: Last four digits of credit card number.

Expiration Date: Credit card expiration date.

Customer Email: Customer email address.

Phone 1: Customer phone number.

Recurring Payment Summary
This report shows a summary of the processed transactions of the recurring payments grouped by payment type:

![](https://files.readme.io/b5d7352207a5060cbbeb6dac41db52474c5fec9fdf33aaf0d6779af72fd12b04-cf4cf9ad856cbab0a361af1ad51464d0a15e948f3ad38d33342963c9f00679f2-image.png)

<br />

Transaction type: product type used in the transaction (ACH or Credit card Company).

Approved Count: Number of approved transactions.

Approved Amount: Sum of approved transactions.

Rejected Count: Number of declined transactions.

Rejected Amount: Sum of declined transactions.

Total Count: Number of transactions.

Total Account: Total sum of transactions.
