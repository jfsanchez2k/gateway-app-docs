Customer Information:
Customer Account: Customer ID number (if the customer has previously processed transactions in the system, entering the customer number and pressing the search icon to the right of the field will populate the customer's information and display the payment methods saved in the customer's wallet).

Register Client: Allows you to register the client when clicked.

Client Name: Client name.

Phone number: Customer's phone number.

Email: Customer email.

City: City Address.

State: State Directorate

CEP: postal area code.

Invoice Information
Invoice number: Invoice number.

Invoice Date: Date of the invoice.

Description: Description of the invoice.

Amount: Invoice amount.

Payment Methods: Selector of payment methods allowed on the invoice (ACH, CC).

Attach file: Allows you to attach files to the invoice.

After filling out the required fields, click the Save button to complete the invoice creation.

An invoice review modal will appear with the previously entered data:
